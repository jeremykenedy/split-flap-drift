# Artwork and screenshot provenance

The cards, digits, reflections, panel, and background are drawn at runtime with Android Canvas. No photos, video, external fonts, or third-party visual assets are bundled. The app icon, TV banner, and DreamService preview are original vector resources.

`docs/screenshots/split-flap-android-tv-api31-1920x1080.png` is an unmodified ADB capture of `PreviewActivity` on the Google Android TV API 31 emulator at 1920 x 1080. The preview ran for more than 45 seconds before the capture. The image shows only the running clock scene, without an app menu, watermark, or overlay. Emulator screenshots do not establish physical display resolution or performance.
